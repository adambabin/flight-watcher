At a glance

Finished departures only. "First seen" uses only releases the tracker caught happening (it was watching while seats were still closed); "3d before" means 3 days before departure.

Day by day

One row per departure. Green bars are when GoWild seats were open; the dark tick is departure; the thin line is when the tracker was watching. A bar with a dotted left end was already open when watching began. Tap a row to open that day above.

Week over week

Each point is one week of departures (Mon–Sun). Hours open counts every window, including seats that came back; a departure that never opened counts as 0. A hollow point is the current week, still in progress.
